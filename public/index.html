Before you order

How do I order?

Add pieces to your cart, press Checkout, fill in your details and submit your order. Your order is saved in our database with a unique tracking ID and confirmed directly on WhatsApp.

How much is delivery?

60 EGP anywhere across all 27 Egyptian governorates. Free for orders of 1,500 EGP or more.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. You can also pay by InstaPay or Vodafone Cash (and online Card / Mobile Wallet via Paymob when enabled).

Is there a discount code?

Join the gang above and code GANG10 (10% off your first order) is applied to your cart automatically, or type it in the promo box inside your cart.

What if the size doesn’t fit?

Message us within 7 days and we’ll exchange it.
