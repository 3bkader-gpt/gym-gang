How do I place an order?

Pick your sizes and add items to your bag, click Proceed to Checkout, fill in your Egypt delivery address, and tap Send Order on WhatsApp. Our team confirms every order directly.

How much is shipping across Egypt?

Flat 60 EGP across Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, and all governorates within 2–4 business days. Orders at or above 1,500 EGP unlock free delivery.

What payment methods are accepted?

You can choose Cash on Delivery, InstaPay, or Vodafone Cash inside checkout (transfer details are shared directly when confirming on WhatsApp).

What if the size does not fit right?

Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with unworn gear and we will arrange a quick size exchange.
